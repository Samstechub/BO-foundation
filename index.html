Why B.O Foundation exists

One life.
A lasting legacy.

B.O Foundation is named in honour of the late Bernadette Olesia, known as “Benna.” A talented and promising rugby player, Benna believed in using sport as a pathway to personal growth, opportunity and an honest living.

After her passing on 15 August 2023 , her family, friends and colleagues came together to preserve and advance the values she lived by.

Bernadette “Benna” Olesia

The woman behind the Foundation

A rugby journey built on dedication and hard work.

Benna's rugby journey began through the Shamas Rugby Foundation , which supported her education while nurturing her sporting talent.

Through dedication and hard work, she progressed to play semi-professional rugby in South Africa and proudly represented Kenya as a member of the Kenya Lionesses Rugby Team .

Her story was one of opportunity, determination and a belief that sport could open doors to a better future.

Her legacy

She believed in giving others a chance too.

Beyond her sporting achievements, Benna was deeply committed to community service.

She dedicated much of her free time to mentoring young boys and girls, providing coaching support and inspiring young people to pursue sport as a pathway to personal growth and development.

Her commitment to young people is at the heart of the Foundation that now carries her name.

A legacy carried forward

Her journey ended too soon.
Her purpose continues.

Following Benna's untimely passing, her family, friends and colleagues resolved to preserve and advance her legacy by establishing B.O Foundation — a foundation committed to empowering young people through sport, education, mentorship and community development.

Our purpose

Creating opportunities that can change lives.

The Foundation works to advance sport, education and community development while creating meaningful opportunities for deserving and disadvantaged young people.

Sports & Culture

To advance sports and culture in ways and places determined by the Foundation, creating opportunities for participation, growth and development.

Education

To advance education and provide educational opportunities to deserving beneficiaries.

Bursaries & Scholarships

To identify and provide bursaries, scholarships and educational materials to disadvantaged persons and communities.

Community Support

To support disadvantaged people and communities, including access to essential support such as shelter where appropriate.

Girls at the heart of our work

Building confidence, opportunity and belonging.

Our focus

Helping girls discover what they can become.

Inspired by Benna's own journey, we believe girls should have access to safe spaces, sport, education, positive role models and opportunities that help them build confident futures.

Rugby remains an important part of this work — but the bigger goal is helping young people develop beyond the playing field.

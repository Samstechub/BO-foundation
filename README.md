# B.O Foundation Website

Tagline: **Hope. Opportunity. A Better Future.**

The homepage is built around the story of the late Bernadette “Benna” Olesia and the purpose of B.O Foundation. The visitor journey is:

**Meet the Foundation → Benna's story → Her legacy → Why the Foundation exists → What it does → Girls at the heart → What support can make possible → Support**

## Files
- `index.html` — homepage
- `contact.html` — contact page
- `donate.html` — donation page
- `assets/site.css` — shared styles
- `assets/images/` — photographs
- `js/script.js` — navigation/header behavior

## Editing
HTML content is kept directly in the page files and sections are separated with clear comments. Main colors are at the top of `assets/site.css`.

Before launch, add the client's verified logo, contact details, donation/payment information, social links, registration details and any approved impact statistics.

The homepage deliberately avoids repeatedly asking for donations. It tells the story first and makes support available after visitors understand the mission.
